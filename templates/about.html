The Four Classified Brain MRI Categories

Glioma

Originates from glial cells (astrocytes, oligodendrocytes, ependymal cells). Can range from low-grade astrocytoma to Grade IV Glioblastoma.

Key MRI Signs: Infiltrative borders, variable ring contrast enhancement, significant surrounding vasogenic edema on T2/FLAIR.
Highest clinical urgency for neurosurgical evaluation.
Meningioma

Primary tumor arising from arachnoid 'cap' cells of the meninges. Predominantly extra-axial and benign (>80%), but can cause focal mass effect.

Key MRI Signs: Well-circumscribed, extra-axial, homogeneous intense gadolinium enhancement with a pathognomonic dural tail sign.
Monitored via serial MRI or resected if symptomatic.
Pituitary Adenoma

Neoplasms located in the sella turcica originating from pituitary neuroendocrine cells. May be functioning (secretory) or non-functioning.

Key MRI Signs: Sellar enlargement, suprasellar extension, compression or draping of the optic chiasm.
Requires visual perimetry & endocrine hormone panel.
No Tumor / Healthy

Normal neuroanatomy without evidence of intracranial mass lesions, abnormal midline shift, pathological enhancement, or ventricular distortion.

Key MRI Signs: Normal cerebral cortex, symmetric ventricular size, distinct grey-white matter junctions.
Baseline neurological health verification.
Why MobileNetV2 for Medical Vision?

MobileNetV2 introduces inverted residual blocks and depthwise separable convolutions, dramatically lowering computational FLOPs and parameter footprint while retaining high discriminatory power:

  • Depthwise Separable Convolutions: Splits standard 2D convolution into depthwise spatial filtering followed by 1x1 pointwise projection, slashing parameters by ~8x.
  • Linear Bottlenecks: Prevents non-linearities (like ReLU) from destroying manifold feature information in low-dimensional compression channels.
  • Sub-100ms Inference: Allows point-of-care deployment directly on commodity workstations without requiring expensive high-end GPU clusters.
